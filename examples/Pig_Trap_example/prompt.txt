Claude Opus 5.5

Design a pig launcher assembly with an NPS 8 minor barrel at least 2 feet long, a NPS 12 major barrel at least 
8 feet long, a NPS 8 kicker line off to the left, a NPS 3 pull port, NPS 1 high point vents, NPS 2 low point 
drains. The kicker should go off to the left when viewed behind the closure (which is a 12" blind flange). 
All flanges should be 600#. Fittings 1" and smaller are 3000# socket connections. Piping 3" and larger should 
be Schedule-STD, 2" and smaller should be Schedule-XS.
Reference @reference/pig_trap_guidelines.md , @reference/Trap_diagram.svg , @docs/freecad-quetzal-guide.md
